About

The team that makes recycling work.

SyncuLa is a Vientiane-based recycling collection and waste-management company. We are present on the ground, clear about the process, and serious about where materials go.

Transparent

Clear prices, clear process, and honest claims — we never publish what we cannot verify.

Traceable

Materials are directed into appropriate recovery streams, not just collected and forgotten.

Safe & responsible

Our teams work safely, and our partners are chosen for responsible handling.
