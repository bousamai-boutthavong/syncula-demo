03 · FAQ

Common questions

Is there a minimum pickup quantity?
We will confirm the current minimum with you in chat — it depends on your location and material type.
Which districts do you cover?
We serve Vientiane Capital. Tell us your district in the pickup request and the team will confirm scheduling.
How do I prepare my materials?
Keep them clean, dry, and separated by type. Flatten cardboard and cartons, and empty all bottles.
How is the price decided?
Prices follow our reference list and are confirmed on inspection, based on material condition and market movement.
How do I get paid?
The team confirms payment details directly with you when the pickup is scheduled.
Can my organisation partner with SyncuLa?
Yes — see About & Partners for collaboration areas, or write to our official partner contact.
